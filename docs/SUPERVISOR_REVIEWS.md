# Supervisor Review of Session Reports

A supervisor reads the reports of the trainees they oversee and adds a review:
a comment, an optional flag for follow-up, and an optional adjusted overall
score. The AI report is never changed. The adjusted score is stored on its own,
with its author and time, and shown beside the AI score.

## Who sees what

| Session | Who reads the report and transcript | Who may review |
|---|---|---|
| Practice (patient library, courses, ladder) | Admins, and supervisors linked to the trainee | The same |
| Skill test | Admins, and the supervisor who assigned the test (`docs/SKILL_TESTS.md`) | The same |

- The superadmin links a supervisor to trainees at `/admin/supervisors`
  (`public.supervisor_trainees`, admin-written). A link never opens another
  supervisor's skill test.
- Revoking the supervisor role removes that supervisor's links, and with them
  their access. Reviews they wrote stay readable to admins.
- Trainees do not see reviews, adjusted scores, or who supervises them. Reports
  stay admin- and supervisor-only, as before.
- Accounts awaiting approval read and write nothing here (the restrictive
  `Approved accounts only` policy covers both tables).

## The review (`public.session_report_reviews`)

- One review per reviewer per session; the reviewer edits or removes their own.
  Admins read every review and may remove one, but cannot rewrite it.
- A review needs at least one of: a comment, an adjusted score, a flag.
- The adjusted score is a whole number from 0 to 100 and needs a comment that
  explains it.
- A review can be written only once the report exists, and only by someone who
  may read that report. The author, session and creation time cannot change.
- Everyone who may review a report sees the other reviews of it. A supervisor
  sees another reviewer's name only when they could already read that profile;
  otherwise the page says "Another supervisor".
- `session_reports` is not touched. `supervisor-reviews.test.ts` checks that the
  review and link routes never write it, and the database checks prove a
  reviewer cannot update it.

## Screens

- `/supervise`: **Trainees you supervise** (linked trainees) above the skill
  tests.
- `/supervise/trainees/[id]`: the trainee's practice sessions with the AI score
  and the supervisor's own review status.
- `/supervise/sessions/[sessionId]`: the report, the review panel, the practice
  panels and the transcript. A skill test session redirects to its test page.
- `/supervise/[id]` (skill test results): a review panel under each report.
- `/admin/reports/[sessionId]`: every review, read-only.
- `/admin/supervisors`: link and unlink trainees for each supervisor.

## API

- `PUT /api/supervise/reviews/[sessionId]` `{ comment, adjustedOverall, flagged }`
  saves the caller's review; `DELETE` removes it. Supervisors and admins only,
  60 changes per hour. Codes: `comment_invalid`, `comment_too_long`,
  `score_invalid`, `comment_required`, `flag_invalid`, `review_empty` (400),
  `review_forbidden` (403), `review_not_found` (404).
- `POST /api/admin/supervisors/trainees` `{ supervisorId, traineeId, link }`
  links or unlinks. Admins with two-step login. Codes: `NOT_SUPERVISOR`,
  `LINK_NOT_UPDATED` (409).

## Not in this version

- Per-item adjusted scores (only the overall score is adjusted).
- Cohorts or institution scoping: links are person to person.
- A heuristic fallback report regenerated by an admin keeps its earlier
  reviews; read the review time against the report.
- Scores, AI or adjusted, are not validated.
