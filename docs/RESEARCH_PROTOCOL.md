# Research Protocol — Observational Use

**Status:** Scientific validation remains **observational** (Stage 8).  
**Do not claim criterion validity or OSCE co-validation.**

## Allowed

- Observational realism ratings  
- Inter-rater agreement collection  
- De-identified export via admin research routes (policy-controlled)  
- Institutional feedback tagged `researcher`

## Forbidden

- Publishing competency scores as validated instruments  
- Re-identifying trainees contrary to IRB/local policy  
- Writing research findings back into patient mind stores

## Metrics (see `PRODUCTION_METRICS.md`)

Inter-rater agreement, DSM/ICD catalog consistency checks, expert agreement via feedback, case/behavior/conversation realism ratings, outcome stability via quality ledger, benchmark suites when authorized.
