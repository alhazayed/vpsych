# Reviewer Agreement — Professional Preview 1.0

**Version:** 1.0  
**Product:** VPsych Educational Simulation Platform  
**Acceptance:** Recorded via `POST /api/ppp/enroll` (`agreement_version = 1.0`) or written acknowledgment to the Release Manager.

By participating as an invited reviewer, you agree that:

1. **Purpose.** You evaluate educational fitness of an AI standardized-patient simulator — not clinical care of real patients.  
2. **No PHI.** You will not enter real patient identifiers, clinical notes about living persons, or other protected health information.  
3. **Fictional patients.** Avatars and cases are synthetic. Treat them as standardized patients for training.  
4. **Formative scores.** Any AI competency scores you see (typically admin-only) are formative. They are **not** validated certification instruments.  
5. **Confidentiality.** Do not publicly redistribute unpublished transcripts, internal reports, or unpublished indices without Release Manager approval.  
6. **Feedback license.** You grant VPsych a non-exclusive license to use your de-identified ratings, CQI reports, and educational opportunity notes to improve the product and for research summaries.  
7. **No endorsement.** Participation does not imply endorsement of VPsych as “validated,” “FDA-cleared,” or “most realistic.”  
8. **Safety.** If you observe a Critical safety or data-integrity defect, report it immediately via CQI (`severity: critical`) and the Release Manager channel.  
9. **Voluntary.** You may withdraw from the preview at any time by notifying the Release Manager.  
10. **Governing docs.** Evaluation follows `EVALUATION_GUIDE.md`, `FEEDBACK_POLICY.md`, and `KNOWN_LIMITATIONS.md`.
