# Publication Roadmap — Evidence Before Claims

## Principle

No paper, landing page, or sales deck may call VPsych “validated,” “proven,” or “the most realistic” psychiatric training simulator until the evidence gates below are met and recorded.

## Stages

| Stage | Evidence | Allowed public language |
|---|---|---|
| **S0 — RC1 Preview** | Wave 3 functional baseline; invited experts | “Professional Preview,” “formative scores,” “under expert evaluation” |
| **S1 — Cohort ratings** | ≥30 session ratings across ≥10 reviewers; CQI triage log | Aggregate Likert summaries with N and caveats |
| **S2 — Reliability** | Expert-scored calibration corpus; ICC / weighted kappa for key rubrics | “Preliminary inter-rater reliability” with coefficients |
| **S3 — Educational outcome** | Pre/post or OSCE-linked study with ethics approval | “Associated with improvement in …” (specific endpoint) |
| **S4 — Comparative realism** | Pre-registered blind protocol vs human SP | Comparative claims limited to measured endpoints |
| **S5 — Peer review** | Accepted manuscript / preprint with open methods | Cite DOI; retire unsupported marketing |

## Explicitly deferred

- Regulatory device claims  
- Diagnostic accuracy guarantees  
- Replacement of human supervision
