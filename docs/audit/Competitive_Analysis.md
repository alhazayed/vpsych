## Competitive Advantages (real today)

1. Bilingual clinical authorship model.  
2. Case Engine nosology separation.  
3. ACE+CGE educational depth uncommon in AI chat demos.  
4. Security posture stronger than typical early AI demos.  
5. Honest internal documentation culture (when followed).

## Competitive disadvantages (real today)

1. Authenticity unproven vs human SP programs.  
2. Excellence narrative ahead of production deployment.  
3. Assessment validity lagging traditional OSCE.  
4. Enterprise procurement checklist incomplete.  
5. Brand presence / AEO weak.
