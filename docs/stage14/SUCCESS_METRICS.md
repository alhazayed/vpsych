# Success Metrics & Longitudinal Trends

**CIDP:** `buildCidpSuccessMetrics()`  
**Phase 14 trends:** `buildSuccessTrends()` · `src/lib/ops/phase14-trends.ts`

## Longitudinal indicators

Institution Adoption · Resident Retention · Faculty Adoption · Session Completion · Assessment Reliability · Supervisor Consistency · Clinical Realism · Platform Availability · System Performance · Support Volume · Critical Incident Rate · Security Events · Research Participation · Certification Success

## Direction semantics

For inverted series (support volume, critical incidents, security events), decreasing values are reported as healthier (`direction: up`).

## Caution

Assessment reliability and clinical realism remain **observational** — not validated instruments.
