# Phase 14 Risk Management

**Code:** `src/lib/ops/phase14-risk-register.ts`  
**Living log:** `../cidp/evidence/risk/RISK_REGISTER.md`  
**Scope:** Product, ops, security, educational, research, and clinical-governance risks.  
**Not in scope:** Patient clinical risk flags (`docs/clinical/RISK_MODEL.md`).

## Required fields

Description · Likelihood · Impact · Owner · Mitigation · Status · Review Date · Escalation Path · Category

## Critical-tier definition

`impact = severe` **or** `(likelihood_score × impact_score) ≥ 15`.

Any critical-tier risk in status `open` or `mitigating` contributes to GA gate `no_unresolved_critical_findings`.

## Executive summary

Generated by `summarizeRiskRegister()` and surfaced on `/admin/cidp` and weekly operations reports.

## Review cadence

- Weekly during active pilots  
- Immediately after Critical feedback or security incidents  
- Before any GA Release Board motion
