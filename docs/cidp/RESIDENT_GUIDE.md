# Resident Guide — Controlled Institutional Deployment

**Audience:** Residents · trainees · therapists in training  
**Version:** `1.0.0-rc.1`

## Login

1. Open the institutional VPsych URL (production or approved pilot URL).  
2. Sign up / sign in with your invited account.  
3. Set preferred language (English or Arabic).  
4. Confirm you will **not** enter real patient information.

## Training workflow

1. Browse the **Patient Library**.  
2. Start a simulation (text recommended first; voice optional).  
3. Practice alliance, history, risk inquiry, and formulation language.  
4. End the session when finished (do not wait for hard expiry unless needed).  
5. Review learning path under **My Learning** and Competency Graph.  
6. Use **Supervisor AI** for counseling-style educational supervision (therapist-focused).

## Clinical simulations

- Patients are **fictional standardized patients**.  
- Each session mints a fresh clinical case; the avatar does not permanently “own” a disorder.  
- If the AI falls back to a persona reply, the UI should show `aiSource` — treat fallbacks honestly.

## Feedback you receive

- Full scoring reports are **admin-only** by design.  
- You may receive formative coaching via learning / supervisor surfaces.  
- Ask faculty for debrief when your program provides it.

## Portfolio & progress

- Learning and competency graph views track formative progress.  
- Build a reflective portfolio with faculty guidance.  
- Progress metrics are educational — not board scores.

## Certification

- Completing simulations does **not** confer medical licensure or board certification.  
- Institutional certificates (if issued) are program-defined overlays.

## Submitting pilot feedback

Use `/feedback` (role: **resident**). Describe issues with severity and steps to reproduce. Never include real names, MRNs, or clinical PHI.
